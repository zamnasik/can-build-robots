// 01 WHY INNOVATEX

Talent is everywhere. Exposure is not.

Many students in rural and semi-urban schools first meet microcontrollers, sensors and robotics only at university, when peers from big cities are already years ahead. InnovateX closes that gap early.

🌍

Innovation is for everyone

It is not only for A/L students or those strong in maths and science. Arts, commerce, technology and science students all find a place in a team.

🛠️

Learn by building

Every concept is taught through a live circuit, a program and a working device students hold in their own hands.

🚀

A culture, not a one-off

Where the programme has run, a real robotics and innovation culture has grown inside the school and the surrounding village.

Our goal is simple: show every student how electronics, embedded systems and code can solve real problems around them.
// 02 PROGRAMME STRUCTURE

How it works in your school

A proven five-stage model, refined over three editions.

Whole-school awakening

One inspiring session for Grades 9 to A/L: what innovation is, who can do it, and what fields it covers.

Teachers' dialogue

An open discussion with interested teachers on science, innovation and how they can support students.

Open application

Students apply for the robotics course. Interest and curiosity matter more than marks.

Team formation

About 100 students form 10 teams of 10. Each team gets a mentor volunteer and a shared kit.

7-week course

Full-day hands-on sessions and a final project showcase in week 7.

// A TYPICAL SESSION DAY

8:30 AM to 3:30 PM, mostly hands-on

CONCEPT

Short, visual presentation of the day's idea with real-world examples.

DEMO

Live demonstration of the circuit and code by the instructor.

BUILD

Teams wire, program and test using their own Arduino / ESP32 kits.

CHALLENGE

A mini problem to solve together, with mentors guiding each team.

// 03 THE 7-WEEK ROADMAP

From first LED to a working smart system

Each week has one clear aim and ends with something the students can show.

WEEK 01

Embedded Systems & Microcontroller Fundamentals

Aim: understand what a microcontroller is and make it respond. Students meet the Arduino, breadboards and basic electronics, then write their first programs.

Arduino UnoLEDs & buttonsBreadboardingFirst code
WEEK 02

Sensors, Actuators & Automation

Aim: let the machine sense and act. Students read the world with sensors and respond with buzzers, servos and relays.

UltrasonicPIR motionDHT11 / DHT22Servo & relay
WEEK 03

Robotics & Autonomous Systems

Aim: build a moving robot. Teams assemble a 4WD car, drive its motors, and make it avoid obstacles on its own.

4WD chassisL298N / L293DObstacle avoidance
WEEK 04

Communication & Wireless Control

Aim: control things without wires. Students learn serial communication and Bluetooth, and drive their robot from a phone.

SerialHC-05 BluetoothMobile app control
WEEK 05

Internet of Things with ESP32

Aim: connect devices to the internet. Students use ESP32 Wi-Fi to send sensor data to a cloud dashboard and control devices from a web page.

ESP32 / ESP8266Wi-FiCloud monitoringWeb control
WEEK 06

RFID, Smart Security & Home Automation

Aim: design real smart-world solutions: an RFID door lock, gas and smoke alarm, rain and soil monitoring, and smart switching.

RFID RC522MQ-2 gasSoil & rainOLED / LCD
WEEK 07

Integrated Project & Showcase

Aim: solve a real problem. Each team designs, builds and presents its own innovation to teachers, parents and guests. Certificates for every participant.

Team projectProblem-solvingPresentationCertificates
